Catch the falling fruit

Use the arrow keys to move the basket. Catch 15 fruits to win. Press start when you're ready.

How to play

Move the basket under the falling fruit. Every fruit you catch adds one point.

How to win

Reach 15 catches before you miss 8 fruits. Watch out for insects, because they reduce your score.

Power-ups

Catch stars for a temporary bonus: bigger basket, faster movement, or a bug clear that removes insects.
